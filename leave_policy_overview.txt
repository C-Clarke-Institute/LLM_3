**Leave Policy Overview**

1. **Payment for Time Not Worked**: This category includes various types of leave and compensation for employees when they are not working:
   - **Paid Holiday**: According to the Factories Act of 1948, adult workers are entitled to weekly paid holidays, preferably on Sundays. If a worker is deprived of their weekly holiday, they are eligible for compensatory holidays in the same month.
   - **Shift Premium**: Companies that operate second and third shifts may pay a premium to workers required to work during night shifts.
   - **Holiday Pay**: Organizations typically offer double the normal salary rate to workers who work during holidays.
   - **Paid Vacation**: Workers in manufacturing, mining, and plantations who have worked for 240 days in a calendar year are eligible for paid vacation at the rate of one day for every 20 days worked for adult workers, and one day for every 15 days worked for child workers.

2. **Employment Rules**: Employees should be familiar with specific workplace rules, including dress code, discipline procedures, parking, attendance, working hours, holidays, employee benefits, and payroll dates. The HR policy on these issues should be easily accessible to employees who have questions or are new to the organization.

3. **Workplace Safety**: Safety policies address worker safety and workplace violence. These policies should be discussed in detail with employee groups to ensure effectiveness, especially regarding emergency procedures. Having a safety policy also helps in adhering to federal and state guidelines for workplace safety.
